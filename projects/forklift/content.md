## The Problem

Autonomous warehouse navigation requires high-precision motor control and constant health monitoring to prevent collisions in tight spaces.

## The Solution

Developing the PID control firmware for precise movement and a C++ and Python-based telemetry interface that monitors the system’s health in real-time, ensuring safe and predictable navigation.
