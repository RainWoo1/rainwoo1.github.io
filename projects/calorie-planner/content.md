## The Problem

Manual food logging is the biggest barrier to consistent nutritional tracking due to the high friction of searching for individual items.

## The Solution

I built a React Native app that allows users to log meals naturally through voice. I developed a Python backend using SpaCy for Named-Entity Recognition (NER) to extract food items and quantities from speech, mapping them to structured datasets instantly.
