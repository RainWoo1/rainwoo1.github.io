ABOUT

I started in art. I ended up in engineering.

Before studying Computer Engineering, I studied design at Yewon School. I still think visually: diagrams, interfaces, and the shape of a system are usually how I start making sense of something complicated.

These days I spend most of my time around robotics software and systems. I like problems where the abstraction leaks a little — where performance, data movement, hardware, or the person using the tool changes the answer.

Outside engineering, I draw, hike, box, golf, and go to classical concerts.
