About

Hey 👋 my name is Jimin! My passion for building things drives my interest in computers, software, and machine learning.

As a Computer Engineering student at the University of Toronto, I seek to combine my determination to solve complex problems with my curiosity about how and why things work. Whether it is building an autonomous mobile robot, working on the Graphcal User Interface system of a self-driving car, or using computer vision to solve complex medical tasks, I'm always interested in learning about how I can apply my knowledge to practical issues around me. Through my work, I have come to understand the principles of engineering design, teamwork, and above all, the importance of creating a system for feedback. What makes my approach unique is my background in fine arts. Graduating at the Design program at Yewon School taught me to decompose complex information into intuitive logical visual logic. I now apply those same principles to software architecture design.

If you want to learn more about my experiences, discuss potential collaborations, or just want to chat about things related to ML and robotics, feel free to contact me!
